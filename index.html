WHAT WE DO

Keep the details of field work connected.

When asset information, site observations and follow-up live in different places, it takes extra effort to understand what changed and what needs attention.

JoLog brings those details together in a location-aware workspace designed around the way field teams and operations staff work.

01

Find

See assets and important site information in their geographic context.

02

Record

Keep inspection and maintenance observations organized by place and time.

03

Follow up

Give teams a shared view of open work and the next action to take.

ABOUT JOLOG

Technology shaped by the realities of the field.

JoLog Technologies is a geospatial software initiative based in Port Harcourt, Nigeria. We are interested in making practical digital tools for organizations that manage people, assets and services across real places.

Our work starts with listening to operators, understanding existing workflows and building around the conditions teams actually face.
